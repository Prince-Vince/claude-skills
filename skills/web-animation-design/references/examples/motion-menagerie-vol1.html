01 · Fade-Up Reveal

The foundational pattern.

Elements translate up 24–30px while fading in. Applied to headings, paragraphs, and content blocks. Uses IntersectionObserver to add a .visible class. Transition runs once, then the class sticks.

First block — observes when it enters the viewport.
Second block — staggers naturally because it enters later.
Third block — same pattern, different content.
02 · Staggered Cascade

Grid cards with a cascade.

Cards share the same reveal, but each has a --i custom property driving transition-delay: calc(var(--i) * 120ms). Wave effect with no JS stagger logic.

03 · Timeline Draw-In

A line that draws as you scroll.

Vertical progress line grows from 0 to full height based on how far you've scrolled through the timeline container. Dots pop in (scale 0→1) when each step becomes visible.

Step one

Line starts drawing as the section enters the viewport.

Step two

Step three

Step four

04 · Counter Roll-Up

Numbers that count up.

Each stat counts from 0 to its target over ~1.4s with cubic ease-out. Triggered once by IntersectionObserver. Uses data-count and data-suffix.

05 · Heading Underline Wipe

A coral line wipes in.

The ::after pseudo-element animates from width 0 to 80px when the heading becomes visible. Small gesture, outsized payoff on section titles.

06 · Scroll Progress Bar

Already active at the top of this page.

The thin coral bar under the nav fills left-to-right as you scroll. Driven by scrollY / (docHeight - innerHeight) via transform: scaleX() with transform-origin: left — GPU-friendly.

07 · Card Hover Lift

Hover to lift.

Subtle translateY(-6px) plus a deeper shadow on hover. Restrained — no rotation, no bounce. Sells "interactive" without feeling cartoony.

08 · Soft Parallax

Hero background drifts.

Background translates at ~30% of scroll speed. Kept deliberately subtle — 20–40px of total movement. JS updates a --scroll custom property inside rAF.
