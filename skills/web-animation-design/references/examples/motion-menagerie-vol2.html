09 · Magnetic Button

The cursor pulls the button.

Within ~120px, the button translates toward the cursor by 30% of the offset. On mouseleave, it smoothly snaps back. Ideal for hero CTAs.

10 · Split-Word Reveal

Words that rise into place.

Each word is wrapped in an inline-block span with overflow:hidden. The inner span translates from translateY(110%) to 0, staggered 80ms per word.

11 · Text Scramble

 

Characters cycle through random symbols before resolving one by one. Uses requestAnimationFrame; finishes in ~1.2s.

12 · Infinite Marquee

A ticker that never stops.

Content is duplicated and a CSS keyframe translates the track by -50%. Seamless loop. Hover to pause.

13 · 3D Tilt Card

Parallax in hover form.

Mouse position maps to rotateX/rotateY with a max of ±10deg. A radial-gradient gloss tracks the cursor inside the card.

14 · Sticky Card Stack

Cards that stack on scroll.

Each card has position: sticky with a cascading top offset. As you scroll, they collect into a deck.

15 · SVG Path Draw-on-Scroll

A line that writes itself.

stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset animate as the section's progress through the viewport changes.

16 · Ripple Click

Feedback from the fingertips.

Click the button — a circular ripple spawns at the click point, scales up, fades, and removes itself. Multiple concurrent ripples supported.
