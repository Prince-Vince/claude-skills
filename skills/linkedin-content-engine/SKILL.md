# LinkedIn content engine

## Inputs
Company services, audience roles, geographic market, website resources, publishing cadence, and authentic expertise. Confirm product claims and case-study permissions.

## Workflow
1. Prioritize three content pillars: buyer problems, walkthroughs, and evidence-based operator perspectives.
2. Build a four-week calendar with a business goal, post format, opening hook, tangible teaching point, source link, CTA, and owner for each post.
3. Draft posts in the company's voice: clear, practical, non-hyped; minimize claims without verification.
4. Where suitable, transform one original website article into: a company post, a personal perspective, a concise PDF carousel outline, and a demonstration storyboard.
5. Separate **illustrative simulations** from real measured client deployments and obtain permission for identifiable customer references.
6. Create distinct UTM-tagged links so referral analytics can attribute website visits without assuming all AI or social referrals are trackable.
7. Review impressions, qualified comments, click-through visits, and consultation requests. Do not treat likes alone as business outcomes.

## Output
A calendar and ready-to-edit drafts with factual-claim sourcing, clear next action, and a short measurement table.

## Limits
Do not directly post, schedule, or access private LinkedIn analytics unless an authorized connector/tool is available and the user requests the action.
